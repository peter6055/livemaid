# Commercial Use Requests

Some uses of LiveMaid require prior written approval from the Licensor under
the [LiveMaid Source Available License 1.0 (LSAL-1.0)](LICENSE).

## Uses that require approval

The following uses are **not** permitted without prior written approval:

- **Hosted or managed service**: Offering LiveMaid or a modified version as a
  hosted, managed, or cloud service to third parties (SaaS, PaaS,
  API-as-a-service, or similar).
- **Competing hosted service**: Operating any hosted service that competes with
  LiveMaid or any hosted service provided by the Licensor, where such service
  is based on LiveMaid or substantial portions of it.
- **Commercial redistribution**: Redistributing, sublicensing, selling, or
  otherwise commercializing LiveMaid (or derivatives) as part of a product or
  service for external customers.
- **Commercial product**: Building and distributing a commercial product from
  LiveMaid or substantial portions of it.

## Uses that do NOT require approval

- Personal, educational, or research use.
- Non-commercial use.
- Internal organizational or business use where the Software is not offered
  as a service to third parties and is not redistributed as a commercial
  product.
- Private modifications (no obligation to upstream).

## How to request approval

Please open an issue in this repository:

**URL:** <https://github.com/peter6055/livemaid/issues>

Use the title format: `Commercial Use Request: <Organization / Use Case>`

### Required information

Include the following details in your request:

1. **Organization name** — your company or individual name.
2. **Contact person and role** — name and title of the person responsible for
   this request.
3. **Intended use case** — describe clearly how you intend to use LiveMaid.
4. **Distribution / deployment model** — how will the Software be deployed or
   distributed (e.g., internal only, offered to customers, SaaS, on-premises
   product)?
5. **Modifications** — do you intend to modify the source code? If so, describe
   the nature of the modifications.
6. **Timeline and scale** — approximate start date and number of users or
   customers affected.

## Response

All commercial use requests are reviewed at the Licensor's sole discretion.
There is no guaranteed response time or guaranteed approval. Approval, if
granted, will be provided in writing (including via the GitHub issue thread).

Submitting a request does not grant permission to use LiveMaid under Section 2
of the license until explicit written approval is received.
